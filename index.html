Executive summary

Coverage. Hotspawn's LoL lobby returned 264 fixtures across 27 tournaments. 195 have a future kickoff and 69 are stale rows (kickoff already passed, yet Hotspawn still lists them as scheduled). Every one of the 264 appears on this site.

Picks. 154 of 195 upcoming fixtures carry a pick with a probability — 79.0% coverage. Two independent methods produce them:

  • 9 market-priced — a real Pinnacle moneyline exists and was de-vigged. This covers the Worlds 2026 Swiss stage and EMEA Masters, the tournaments the sportsbooks actually price. Browse market-priced picks →
  • 145 model-priced — no bookmaker quotes the match, so the number comes from our Elo rating built on 7579 historical results (1394 teams rated). Browse model-priced picks →

Unresolved. 110 of 264 fixtures have no published number, for one of two stated reasons: 69 are stale rows whose kickoff has passed with no result published anywhere, and 41 have no bookmaker market plus fewer than three rated results for at least one team — below that bar a model output is mostly the 1500-Elo prior. Each row is listed with its exact limitation and what would resolve it on the Unresolved & Limitations page →. Nothing is filled in with a guess.

Sourcing rule. Sportsbooks and prediction markets only. Hotspawn supplies the fixture list and nothing else — its "picked %" is a popularity poll and is deliberately excluded from the probability calculation. Source register →
