Executive summary

  • 264 matches are on the HotSpawn League of Legends board right now and every one of them has a pick and a probability for both teams on the picks page.
  • 9 matches are priced by an external series line (Kalshi, Polymarket, Pinnacle, Bovada - de-vigged). 254 matches are priced by our own Elo line fitted on Leaguepedia results. 1 match has no verifiable history for at least one team and is published as a flat 50% and flagged as unverified.
  • HotSpawn's crowd percentages are never used as a probability signal. HotSpawn is the fixture list only.
  • Walk-forward test of our own line on 399 of the 1814 collected results: 58.9% of the predicted winners were right. Treat small gaps as noise.
  • Limitations: the books only price the big circuits, so most of the board is covered by our own line; low-tier leagues are thinly covered on Leaguepedia, which caps the accuracy of our line there; a team we cannot match to a wiki page gets a flat 50% rather than an invented number. All of these are listed on the unresolved page and the flagged for review page.

Read this before you use a number. A percentage here means one of four things, and the badge says which: a external series line is a de-vigged price from a licensed book or prediction market; a external map price only is a price for a single game, not the series; a our own line is our Elo model fitted on Leaguepedia results; a unverified means we found no price and no result history for at least one team, so the probability is a flat 50% and is flagged as unverified on the Flagged for review page.
Automation. The board is re-collected every 4 hours by a scheduled GitHub Actions run (.github/workflows/refresh.yml): the HotSpawn inventory, the external lines, the reference results, the picks and this site are all rebuilt and committed automatically.

How to read this site. Every match listed on HotSpawn's League of Legends pick'em board gets a pick and a win probability for both teams. Probabilities come from external betting lines (Kalshi, Polymarket, Pinnacle, Bovada) or, where no book prices the match, from our own line fitted on Leaguepedia results. HotSpawn's own "X% picked" figures are never used as a probability signal.
