What this board tells you

  • 171 of 200 upcoming fixtures carry a pick (86%). 9 use a real external line (Kalshi exchange, Pinnacle, Polymarket); 162 use our results-only Elo because no bookmaker prices that league.
  • 93 fixtures are unresolved — 64 are stale Hotspawn entries whose start time has passed but which Hotspawn still lists as scheduled, and the rest lack enough verified match history. Every one is listed with its exact reason and what would fix it. Open the unresolved list.
  • Hotspawn’s crowd percentages are never used. Our probabilities come from de-vigged external market prices and from completed-match results only. How the numbers are built.
  • Results cross-check: 948 of 951 completed matches that appear in both the Hotspawn and Leaguepedia feeds agree on the winner (99.7%); any disagreement is listed on review.
  • Refresh cadence: the collector runs every 6 hours via GitHub Actions (17 */6 * * *). Last successful run 2026-10-21 20:35 UTC; next scheduled run 2026-10-22 02:17 UTC. Source register & status.
  • Limitations: licensed books price only the top leagues and international events. Low-tier national leagues have no market anywhere we could verify, so those probabilities are model-derived and marked low confidence. Flagged for review.
