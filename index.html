Separate unresolved section

Matches without a verified external line

We still show listed matches when books or prediction markets do not quote them. A pick is withheld until independent evidence exists.

01

Low-tier / regional coverage

Books often do not post a moneyline for lower-tier, academy, or regional matches. If no market is found, the winning team and probability stay blank.

02

Market coverage gaps

Polymarket and other public prediction markets list selected events, not every Hotspawn fixture. Market absence does not imply equal chances or a team preference.

03

Line freshness & identity

Odds may open late, suspend in-play, or use a different team spelling. We only match a line when both teams and event identity can be checked; stale or ambiguous matches are flagged.

Method, not crowd consensus

Probabilities come from external match-winner prices.

When supported by market coverage, decimal odds are converted to implied probabilities and normalized across both teams to remove the bookmaker margin. No Hotspawn popularity share enters that calculation. We do not invent results when the market is absent.
