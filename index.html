What this site does

1. It lists every scheduled League of Legends Pick'em on Hotspawn — not just the high-profile ones. The board is read directly from Hotspawn's own fixture API.

2. It prices each match from outside esports betting markets first — Pinnacle, Unibet (Kambi) and Polymarket. Prices are de-vigged into probabilities.

3. Where no market exists, it originates its own line from official Leaguepedia match history using a documented Elo + recent-form model with sample-size shrinkage.

4. Where neither is possible, it says so. Those matches get a nominal 50/50 pick and a row on the Unresolved page with the limitation stated.

What this site does not do

Hotspawn's own crowd pick percentages are never used as a probability input. The "X% picked" figures shown on the Hotspawn lobby are the crowd's picks, not a line. They are recorded on this site for context and clearly labelled, but they never feed a number.

No value is invented. If a number cannot be traced to an official source it is left blank and flagged on the Flagged for Review page.

Unresolved picks — the limitation in one paragraph

Most fixtures on the Hotspawn LoL board are Tier C/D amateur and regional circuits (PRM 3rd Division, Tunisian Super League, RIVALS LEAGUE, Komplettligaen, Arabian regional leagues and similar). No commercial bookmaker and no prediction market prices these fixtures, and many of the teams have no completed match recorded in Leaguepedia — rosters in these circuits turn over constantly and results are frequently unreported. For those matches we publish a nominal 50/50 pick so the board is complete, and we label it Unresolved so it is never confused with a real edge. See the Unresolved page for the full list and the detailed limitations.

How often is this updated?

A GitHub Actions workflow re-runs the whole pipeline every 6 hours (and on demand), re-reads the Hotspawn board, re-pulls every market and the Leaguepedia history, and republishes data/picks.json. The timestamp at the top of this page is the collection time. No manual input is required.

Flagged for review

Items we could not fully verify — reconstructed match permalinks, thin-history model lines, single-book lines and stale board entries — are published with their source links on the Flagged for Review page.
