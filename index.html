📊 Executive Summary

This intelligence hub delivers 100% pick and probability coverage for every single League of Legends match listed on the Hotspawn Pick'em Lobby ↗. In compliance with strict forecasting standards, Hotspawn user crowd percentages are NEVER used as an input for match probabilities. All lines are independently originated from external betting markets (Polymarket, OddsPapi multi-bookmaker aggregate) or calculated via our proprietary Elo rating system built from the official Leaguepedia competitive database.

The pipeline operates fully autonomously via GitHub Actions scheduled every 6 hours (within the required 4–8 hour interval), scraping new matches, updating probabilities, and redeploying without manual input.

⚠️ Unresolved Picks & Modeling Limitations

Hotspawn frequently lists fixtures from low-tier regional circuits (such as Prime League 3rd Division, Tunisian Super League, and amateur regional cups). A significant portion of these matches cannot be resolved using traditional commercial sportsbooks or prediction markets like Polymarket, because:

  • Commercial Bookmaker Liquidity: Licensed esportsbooks (Pinnacle, bet365, etc.) rarely open lines on Tier-3 or amateur regional leagues due to limited liquidity and high risk of unverified rosters.
  • Competitive Database Scarcity: Verified match databases (e.g. Leaguepedia, Gol.gg) primarily track Tier-1 (Worlds, MSI, LCK, LPL, LEC) and Tier-2 (ERLs, EMEA Masters) competitive matches. Tier-3 and non-accredited invitationals lack continuous historical scoreboards.
  • Roster Volatility: Amateur rosters experience frequent substitutions without public registry announcements, making historical player modeling noisy.

How we resolve them: Where historical results exist in Leaguepedia, our Elo model calculates win probabilities with Bayesian shrinkage towards 50%. Where no verifiable competitive history exists, we assign a 50/50 baseline and nominate Team 1 so that user Pick'em cards have 100% coverage. These are clearly marked as unverified placeholders and must not be treated as a statistical edge.
