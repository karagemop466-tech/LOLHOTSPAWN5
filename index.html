Executive summary

Coverage. Hotspawn's LoL lobby returned 232 fixtures across 29 tournaments. 148 have a future kickoff and 84 are stale rows (kickoff already passed, yet Hotspawn still lists them as scheduled). Every one of the 232 appears on this site.

Picks. 144 of 148 upcoming fixtures carry a pick with a probability — 97.3% coverage. Two independent methods produce them:

  • 0 market-priced — a real external moneyline exists (Pinnacle bookmaker, Kalshi exchange, Bovada when it answers) and was de-vigged. This covers the tournaments the books and exchanges actually price. Browse market-priced picks →
  • 144 model-priced — no bookmaker quotes the match, so the number comes from our Elo rating built on 23333 historical results (3982 teams rated). Browse model-priced picks →

Unresolved picks & limitations

Matches we cannot give a defensible pick for — and exactly what is missing.

88 of 232 fixtures are published blank, with the limitation and the remedy on the row. Nothing is filled in with a guess. The limitations fall into two categories:

  • 84 stale fixtures — the kickoff has already passed but Hotspawn still lists the row as scheduled and no source publishes a result. A pick could not be acted on, and any figure would be hindsight.
  • 4 insufficient-history fixtures — no bookmaker, exchange or prediction-market line covers the match, and at least one team has fewer than three verified completed results on Leaguepedia. Below that bar our Elo output is mostly a 1500 prior, i.e. a guess wearing a percentage.
