Executive summary

Coverage. Hotspawn's LoL lobby returned 149 fixtures across 38 tournaments. 54 have a future kickoff and 95 are stale rows (kickoff already passed, yet Hotspawn still lists them as scheduled). Every one of the 149 appears on this site.

Picks. 47 of 54 upcoming fixtures carry a pick with a probability — 87.0% coverage. Two independent methods produce them:

  • 1 market-priced — a real external moneyline exists (Pinnacle bookmaker, Kalshi exchange, Bovada when it answers) and was de-vigged. This covers the tournaments the books and exchanges actually price. Browse market-priced picks →
  • 46 model-priced — no bookmaker quotes the match, so the number comes from our Elo rating built on 23395 historical results (4049 teams rated). Browse model-priced picks →

Sourcing rule. Sportsbooks, exchanges and prediction markets only. Hotspawn supplies the fixture list and nothing else — its "picked %" is a popularity poll and is deliberately excluded from the probability calculation. Source register →

Unresolved picks & limitations

Matches we cannot give a defensible pick for — and exactly what is missing.

102 of 149 fixtures are published blank, with the limitation and the remedy on the row. Nothing is filled in with a guess. The limitations fall into two categories:

  • 95 stale fixtures — the kickoff has already passed but Hotspawn still lists the row as scheduled and no source publishes a result. A pick could not be acted on, and any figure would be hindsight.
  • 7 insufficient-history fixtures — no bookmaker, exchange or prediction-market line covers the match, and at least one team has fewer than three verified completed results on Leaguepedia. Below that bar our Elo output is mostly a 1500 prior, i.e. a guess wearing a percentage.
