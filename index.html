Picks. 32 of 36 upcoming fixtures carry a pick with a probability — 88.9% coverage. Two independent methods produce them:

Unresolved picks & limitations

Matches we cannot give a defensible pick for — and exactly what is missing.

102 of 134 fixtures are published blank, with the limitation and the remedy on the row. Nothing is filled in with a guess. The limitations fall into two categories:

  • 98 stale fixtures — the kickoff has already passed but Hotspawn still lists the row as scheduled and no source publishes a result. A pick could not be acted on, and any figure would be hindsight.
  • 4 insufficient-history fixtures — no bookmaker, exchange or prediction-market line covers the match, and at least one team has fewer than three verified completed results on Leaguepedia. Below that bar our Elo output is mostly a 1500 prior, i.e. a guess wearing a percentage.
