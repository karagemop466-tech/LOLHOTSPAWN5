Methodology

How each number on this site is produced, in the order it is applied.

1. Fixture inventory

The match list is taken from play.hotspawn.com/lol-pickems. That page is an Angular server-rendered app; the complete board is embedded in its ng-state transfer-state script. We parse that payload directly, which is why the inventory is the full 212 fixtures rather than the first page of a rendered list. Fields taken verbatim: match id, tournament, tier, stage, start time, lock time, both team names, their Hotspawn pick share, and the link to the Hotspawn match page.

2. External market lines

We query three external price sources, in priority order — Pinnacle (sharp bookmaker guest API), Kalshi (exchange, series KXLOLGAME, per-match binary moneylines) and Bovada (best-effort). For Pinnacle we take the period-0 moneyline market and convert American odds to implied probability:

p = 100 / (price + 100)        if price > 0
p = -price / (-price + 100)    if price < 0

For Kalshi we take the midpoint of each side's yes bid/ask in dollars. Prices include a margin (vig or spread), so the two implied probabilities sum to more than 1. They are de-vigged proportionally:

p_no_vig(a) = p(a) / (p(a) + p(b))

A Hotspawn fixture only takes a market price when both team names match the external matchup and the two start times agree within 8 hours. When more than one source matches, the highest-priority source wins and the row's source links show which price was used. Any partial match is discarded — a wrong join is worse than no join. Established esportsbooks (GG.BET, BC.GAME, N1 Bet, Melbet, Thunderpick, Rivalry, 1xBet, EGamersWorld) are probed every refresh for low-tier lines; their availability is recorded in the source register, and no price is ever taken from a probe that did not actually return parseable prices.

3. Our own Elo model

For fixtures no bookmaker prices, we rate teams ourselves from Leaguepedia's MatchSchedule results, walking matches in chronological order. K rises with series length, so a BestOf 5 moves ratings further than a BestOf 1:

E(a)        = 1 / (1 + 10^((Rb - Ra) / 400))
Ra'         = Ra + K · (S(a) - E(a))
K           = BestOf 1 → 20, BestOf 2 → 24, BestOf 3 → 28, BestOf 5 → 36
decay       = 0.5 ^ (days_since_last_match / 365)
R_effective = 1500 + (R - 1500) · n / (n + 8)

New teams move faster (K is multiplied by 1.6 until they have 10 results), inactive teams drift back toward the mean, and small samples are shrunk toward 1500 so a 2-match team cannot produce a 95% probability. The probability we publish is E(a) at the shrunk ratings.

4. Confidence tiers

TierRuleMeaning
Market-pricedA matched external moneyline (Pinnacle / Kalshi / Bovada) External price; strongest evidence on the site.
HighBoth teams ≥ 20 rated results Model has a solid sample.
MediumBoth teams ≥ 8 Reasonable, watch for roster churn.
LowBoth teams ≥ 3 Thin sample; treat as indicative only.
Insufficient dataA team has < 3 No probability is published. At that sample size the rating is mostly a prior regressed to 1500 Elo, and a prior is not evidence about these rosters. The row is listed on the Unresolved page with the shortfall named.
No pickKickoff already passed, or < 3 rated results for either team Probability and pick fields are left blank and the fixture is listed on the Unresolved page with what would resolve it.

5. What we deliberately do not do

  • We never use Hotspawn's "picked %" as a probability. It measures how other users voted, not how likely a team is to win. It is shown for transparency only.
  • We never invent a price. If no book quotes a match, the model is labelled as a model, not dressed up as a market.
  • We never backfill a missing value. Blank stays blank and is published on the Flagged for review page.
  • We only use Polymarket or Kalshi when a live per-match market matches a board fixture. Kalshi KXLOLGAME per-match exchange prices are ingested and used like a bookmaker price (de-vigged). Polymarket's open LoL markets have been outright/historical rather than per-match; when that changes they will be used the same way. Outright futures are never converted into a per-match probability.

6. Refresh cadence

A GitHub Actions workflow re-runs the whole pipeline on a 4-hour schedule and redeploys this site, so the board follows Hotspawn's own updates without manual input. Every snapshot carries the collection timestamp shown in the header.
