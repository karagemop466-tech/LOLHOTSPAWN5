Items that could not be fully verified from an official source. Nothing here is filled in or inferred — each entry states exactly what is missing and links to the official record behind it.

Standing irregularities

1. Tier is reported as "WAITING FOR INFORMATION" for most fixtures. This is Hotspawn's own value, copied verbatim from their API. It is not a gap in our data — Hotspawn has not assigned a tier to those competitions. Affected rows show TIER TBC.

2. No betting market exists for most of the board. Pinnacle and Unibet together price roughly a dozen League of Legends fixtures a day, all World Championship / EMEA Masters. The remainder of the board is unpriced anywhere. This is a property of the market, not of the pipeline.

3. Leaguepedia coverage of amateur/regional circuits is partial. Where a team has no completed game in the last 420 days we cannot originate a line and the fixture is marked unresolved.

4. Match permalinks for fixtures outside the server-rendered lobby HTML are reconstructed. The fixture record itself is official (Hotspawn fixture API); the www.hotspawn.com/matches/… URL is built from Hotspawn's public slug pattern and marked unverified, because www.hotspawn.com returns HTTP 403 to the automation runner and the URL could not be confirmed.

5. Some fixtures stay at status scheduled after their start time. These are retained for completeness and flagged as stale, but their pick window has closed.
