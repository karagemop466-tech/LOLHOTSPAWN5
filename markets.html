Market lines

Fixtures priced by an outside esportsbook or prediction market. These are the strongest lines on the site: Pinnacle and Unibet moneylines are de-vigged into probabilities, and Polymarket prediction prices are normalised. Where more than one source prices the same match the de-vigged probabilities are averaged, with Pinnacle weighted double as the sharp book.

Why this tier is small. Commercial books only price top-tier League of Legends. On the current board that is essentially the World Championship and EMEA Masters. Everything else on the Hotspawn board has no market at all — see Originated lines and Unresolved.

Hotspawn crowd percentages are not used. Nothing on this page is derived from the Hotspawn lobby's pick split.
