Originated lines

Fixtures with no betting market, where we originate our own line. A proprietary Elo rating with recent-form blending is computed from completed matches recorded in Leaguepedia (the official League of Legends esports wiki), then shrunk toward 50% in proportion to how thin the underlying match history is.

How the model works, in short. Every completed game involving a board team in the last 420 days is pulled from Leaguepedia's ScoreboardGames Cargo table. Games are replayed in chronological order to produce an Elo rating (start 1500, K = 28). The head-to-head Elo expectation is blended 70/30 with a 90-day win-rate differential, then shrunk toward 50% as n/(n+12) where n is the smaller team's game count.

Known weakness. Amateur rosters change between splits and Leaguepedia coverage of regional circuits is patchy, so ratings for thinly-documented teams are deliberately pulled close to even. Rows built on fewer than 5 games are additionally flagged.
