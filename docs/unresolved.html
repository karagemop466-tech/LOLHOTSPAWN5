⚠️ Unresolved Picks & Modeling Limitations

Transparent audit of low-tier League of Legends matches that cannot be priced via external market lines or historical databases.

🔍 Why Certain Matches Cannot Be Resolved Like Featured Picks

In League of Legends esports, betting markets and predictive models depend heavily on verified competitive data. When examining the full Hotspawn lobby, several dozen matches belong to amateur, semi-pro, or regional Tier-3 circuits (e.g., Prime League 3rd Division 26-27 Fall, Tunisian Super League, and local invitational qualifiers).

Our investigation into multiple information sources yielded the following findings:

  • Established LoL Betting Sites & Esportsbooks (Pinnacle, bet365, Betway, Thunderpick): These platforms only open betting lines for Tier-1 (Worlds, MSI, LCK, LPL, LEC, LCS) and Tier-2 (EMEA Masters, ERL 1st divisions). Tier-3 regional tournaments do not have official lines due to limited betting volume, integrity monitoring constraints, and lack of official Riot API feeds.
  • Esports Prediction Markets (Polymarket): Polymarket covers major international tournaments and top regional finals. Lower-tier matches have no market contracts created.
  • Community Match Databases (Leaguepedia Cargo API, Gol.gg, Strafe): While Leaguepedia maintains an exhaustive competitive database, amateur 3rd Division scoreboards are frequently unrecorded or missing player IDs. Without historical games, traditional Elo or Bradley-Terry models cannot compute a statistical rating.
  • Hotspawn Metadata Status: Many of these matches are marked by Hotspawn itself as "WAITING FOR INFORMATION" for tier assignment, points value, or exact start times.

📋 Concrete Limitations & Pick Resolution Strategy

1. High Roster Volatility

In amateur circuits, teams frequently undergo complete roster overhauls week-to-week without formal announcement. Any historical team rating carries higher uncertainty.

2. Absence of Market Price Discovery

Without sharp market makers (like Pinnacle) setting liquidity lines, there is no external consensus to benchmark against.

3. Our Resolution Framework

To guarantee our users can complete 100% of their Hotspawn Pick'em slips:
• If a team has even 1–2 games in Leaguepedia, our Elo model computes a win probability with heavy Bayesian shrinkage toward 50%.
• If neither team has recorded data, we assign an explicit 50.0% / 50.0% baseline and nominate Team 1. Limitation: This pick represents a coin flip, not a statistical edge.
