The two reasons a row is blank

  • Stale fixture. Hotspawn still lists the match as scheduled although its kickoff has passed, and neither Hotspawn nor Leaguepedia publishes a result. A pick for it could not be acted on, and our ratings already contain results from after that kickoff, so any number would be hindsight.
  • Too little rated history. No bookmaker or exchange prices the match, and Leaguepedia holds fewer than 3 rated results for at least one of the two teams. Under that bar the Elo rating is mostly a 1500-point prior — a guess with a decimal point — so the row stays blank instead of being dressed up as a probability.
