Why this section exists. Most of these matches are low-tier regional leagues. No sportsbook prices them, prediction markets do not list them, and the match history needed to model them is thin or missing. Rather than leaving them out - or publishing a number we cannot defend - we give every one of them a pick, label the basis honestly, and record the limitation here.

The limitations, in full

  • No market price exists. The match is in a league no bookmaker prices, so there is no external line to de-vig. Our line is a model, not a market, and is labelled our own line.
  • Only a single-map price exists. Kalshi publishes a price for one game of the match, not the series. We show the map price and flag it; the series format (best-of-N) is only converted when it is verified from a source, never guessed.
  • No verified match history. Leaguepedia does not publish results for at least one of the two teams, so our model has nothing to fit. Those rows carry a flat 50% and are flagged as unverified on the Flagged for review page.
  • Team-name ambiguity. A few orgs have several entries on Leaguepedia (for example regional sister teams). Where the mapping is ambiguous we flag the row instead of silently choosing one.
