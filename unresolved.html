Unresolved picks & limitations

Fixtures for which no external betting line and no usable Leaguepedia match history exists. These are published with a nominal 50/50 pick so the board is complete, and are never presented as having an edge.

Why these picks cannot be resolved

1. There is no betting market for them. We checked, in order: established esportsbooks (Pinnacle's public esports board, Unibet's Kambi esports feed), then prediction markets (Polymarket's League of Legends markets), then the odds-aggregator APIs (OddsPapi, PandaScore, Abios — all require a paid key we do not hold). Pinnacle and Unibet together price roughly a dozen League of Legends fixtures worldwide on any given day, and they are all World Championship / EMEA Masters. Tier C and Tier D regional and amateur circuits — which is what most of the Hotspawn board is — are not priced anywhere, by anyone.

2. There is no reliable result history either. Leaguepedia is the most complete public record of League of Legends esports, but coverage of the lowest tiers is partial: matches go unreported, team names are inconsistent between splits, and rosters in these circuits turn over every few weeks. Where neither team has a completed game in the last 420 days, there is simply nothing to model.

3. Roster volatility makes any prior signal decay fast. Even where a team does have history, an amateur roster that has turned over since those games were played is a different team. We do not have access to current roster data for these circuits from an official source, so we do not attempt to adjust for it.

What we publish instead, and how to read it

Each unresolved fixture is published as a nominal 50/50 pick: both teams show 50.0%, the listed pick is simply team A (the team Hotspawn lists first), and the row is badged Unresolved. There is no statistical edge behind that pick — it exists only so that the board has a complete row set and so that the gap in coverage is visible rather than hidden.

These rows still carry official source links: the Hotspawn fixture record and the Hotspawn match page, so you can verify that the fixture exists and check its tier, points and lock time yourself.
