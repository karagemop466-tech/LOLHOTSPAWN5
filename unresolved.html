Unresolved picks & limitations

This page exists so that a missing pick is never quietly filled with a guess. It lists exactly which fixtures have no defensible probability, and the specific limitation behind each one.

Why a match can be unresolved

  • No sportsbook market. Pinnacle prices the Worlds 2026 Swiss stage and EMEA Masters. It does not price regional third divisions, national leagues or academy circuits.
  • No match history. Our Elo model needs results. Teams brand-new to the Leaguepedia record have no rating, so no probability can be justified.
  • Stale fixtures. Some rows have a start time in the past while Hotspawn still lists them as scheduled, and carry no Hotspawn match page. Any number for those is unactionable.
  • Prediction markets are not per-match here. Polymarket's League of Legends markets in this snapshot are historical (LCK Challengers from August–September 2026) and Kalshi only lists a Worlds outright winner market — neither can price an individual fixture.
