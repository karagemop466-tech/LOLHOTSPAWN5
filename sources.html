Sourcing rule

Match probabilities may come only from external sportsbooks/prediction markets or from our own documented model built on historical results. Hotspawn supplies the fixture list and nothing else.

Hotspawn LoL Pick Desk — independent analysis of League of Legends esports markets.

Match inventory: Hotspawn Play pick'ems (collected 2026-10-30 20:52:19 UTC). Probabilities: external sportsbook lines where available, otherwise our own Elo model. Hotspawn's crowd pick share is never used as a probability.

Not betting advice. Verify every number against the linked source before acting on it.
