Hotspawn is a source of fixtures, never of probability

Hotspawn is used only to enumerate which matches exist, together with their tournament, tier, points, start/lock times and the crowd's pick split. The crowd's pick split is shown for context and is never an input to a probability on this site.

Hotspawn LoL Pick Desk — independent research dashboard. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Hotspawn.

Match board: play.hotspawn.com/lol-pickems. Data is regenerated automatically every 6 hours by GitHub Actions. Nothing on this site is gambling advice.

Every table row on this site links to the official source it was derived from. Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are left blank and flagged on the Flagged for Review page.
