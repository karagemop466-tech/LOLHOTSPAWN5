What is deliberately not used

  • HotSpawn crowd percentages. The "X% picked" figures on the HotSpawn board are ownership data, not a forecast. They are not collected and never enter the probability model.
  • HotSpawn results. For the same reason the historical results on HotSpawn are not used to fit the model.
  • Unconfirmed values. Where a source does not publish a number, the cell is left blank and the row is flagged rather than filled in with an estimate.

How to read this site. Every match listed on HotSpawn's League of Legends pick'em board gets a pick and a win probability for both teams. Probabilities come from external betting lines (Kalshi, Polymarket, Pinnacle, Bovada) or, where no book prices the match, from our own line fitted on Leaguepedia results. HotSpawn's own "X% picked" figures are never used as a probability signal.
